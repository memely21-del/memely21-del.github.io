How it works

  1. You send the brief

    The product, who it is for, and what you want the video to do. An email is enough.

  2. You approve the script

    Before anything is filmed you get the script and two hook options, in the language you asked for. Nothing gets shot until you say yes.

  3. I film and edit

    Shot at home in natural light, cut, captioned and colour-matched. Within 7 days of the product arriving.

  4. You get the files

    1080×1920 MP4 (not the 720p preview on this page), captions burned in, ready to upload. One revision round included.

Questions brands ask

How is this different from an influencer post?

An influencer posts to their own audience. I hand you the footage and you publish it — on your feed, your ads, your website. You are buying content, not a follower count I do not have yet.

How long does it take?

Seven days from the day the product reaches me. If you need it faster, a three-day rush is possible for 25% more — say so before we start, not after.

Do I own the content?

Yes. Organic use is included in every price: your social accounts, your website, your email. Running it as a paid ad is a separate licence, listed in the rates. Whitelisting needs a public handle — mine are not public yet, so I will not sell that add-on until they are.

Can I really get the same video in English and in Spanish?

Yes. Six tiles above are the same concept in both languages — press ES or EN on those. Spanish is my first language. English is a voice matched to mine, not a second creator. Nizoral has both languages, with a different edit in each.

Do you appear on camera?

Every piece in this portfolio is hands-and-voice: the product, the hands using it, and a voiceover. If your brief needs face-to-camera, say so and I will tell you straight whether it fits.

Do you need me to send the product?

Yes. I film what I can actually hold and use — that is the whole point of UGC. The seven days start when it arrives, not when you order it.

What about revisions?

One round is included in every price: a different hook, a re-cut, a caption fix. Beyond that it is quoted before I touch it, never billed as a surprise.

How do payments work?

Half to book the slot, half when the files land. Invoiced by email.
